Structural edge review

Load annotations.json from this folder. Images contain no model predictions, to reduce annotation bias. Mark visible architectural boundaries only: wall intersections, door openings, and window openings; exclude furniture and texture. Do not draw hidden boundaries.

Click endpoints to draw a polyline, then Finish line. To limit evaluation to an unambiguous region, choose Region and click its two corners. Every visible structural edge inside each reviewed rectangle must be labeled. Unreviewed pixels are ignored.
