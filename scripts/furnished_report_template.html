Rendered with Isaac Sim 5.1.0 on NVIDIA DGX Spark. Furniture combines procedural geometry with CC0 assets from Poly Haven. All images are actual simulator renders. No AI model is trained or used to produce these images.

Labels represent the original room shell. Visibility uses first-hit depth with a 2.5 cm tolerance; mirrors do not add reflected edges to ground truth. Trim can cover the original wall–floor and opening boundaries. The ceiling is hidden only for top-down inspection.

This gallery and all generated media stay in the Git-ignored vis/ folder.
